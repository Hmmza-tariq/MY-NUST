Your connection. Your choice.

Saving portal credentials is optional. They are stored locally in encrypted app preferences and used to sign in directly to Qalam or LMS—not uploaded to Hexagone servers. Cached academic summaries and planner entries stay on your device. You can turn the connection off or choose which saved data to delete in Settings. Optional usage analytics has a separate consent choice and can be disabled in About.
