Privacy policy

This policy covers My NUST, listed on Google Play as “NUST - A Student App,” and this companion website. The app is developed by Hexagone, independently of NUST. Contact us at HexagonePk@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

If you choose to connect a portal, the app saves your student ID and portal passwords in encrypted local preferences. It also caches available academic details, which may include your name, GPA, course history, attendance, timetable, deadlines and invoices. Planner entries, campus/theme preferences and downloaded files are saved locally. Portal credentials and academic summaries are not uploaded to Hexagone servers by the app's sync feature.

Encrypted preferences are not a guarantee against access on a compromised device. Device or operating-system backups may retain copies depending on your platform and backup settings.

Portal access and automatic sync

Saving credentials and enabling the portal connection are optional. With your consent, the app signs in directly to official Qalam or LMS services to read available records on launch, on eligible returns to the app, and when you refresh. These services receive your credentials and read requests over their connections, and their pages may use cookies or browser storage. Their own privacy policies apply. You can use local planners and open official portals without saving credentials.

When you turn the connection off, automatic sync and saved-password autofill stop; saved credentials and cached summaries are kept until you delete them. Turning sync off does not automatically sign you out of an existing web session.

Firebase and other external services

The app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications and Firebase Realtime Database for app information. Firebase may process installation identifiers, notification tokens and technical service information such as IP addresses. This is separate from locally cached academic data. See Firebase's privacy information for Google's handling and retention of service data.

Opening campus pages, downloading files, fetching news or loading remote images sends normal network requests to those providers. The app uses Google Fonts for its interface, which may fetch fonts when they are not cached. Opening Google Play or GitHub uses those services under their policies.

Optional usage analytics

Starting with version 3.0.2, My NUST offers optional Google Analytics for Firebase. Analytics collection is disabled until you explicitly choose “Enable analytics.” From version 3.0.4, the first-launch prompt has no automatic timeout. Analytics stays off while you read and until you explicitly enable it. “Not now,” closing the prompt, or closing its details without enabling leaves analytics off. Your choice is remembered across app launches. Declining does not limit app features. If enabled, Firebase receives screen visits, feature interactions, counts (such as courses added), categorical success/failure results and timing, together with app/device information and an app instance identifier. Google Analytics derives approximate country/region/city information from the connection’s IP address. The app does not request GPS or precise device location for analytics. Portal-opening events identify LMS or Qalam, and news-click events use a public destination domain and an opaque identifier for the public news link. Google also processes technical network information under its service terms. See Google Analytics data-collection information. Advertising storage, ad user data and personalized advertising signals remain disabled; the Android app excludes advertising identifier permissions.

Our custom analytics excludes names, student IDs, portal usernames/passwords, cookies, portal HTML, grades, GPA/SGPA/CGPA values, marks, attendance, invoices, feedback text and private URLs. News click identifiers are derived only from public campus-news links. No separate student account or analytics user ID is assigned by Hexagone.

You can withdraw consent using About → Enable analytics. In version 3.0.2 this control was in Settings. This stops further analytics collection and resets analytics data and the app instance identifier on this device. It does not automatically delete previously processed server-side reports. Contact us with a deletion request; Google’s service retention and deletion mechanisms also apply. Clearing app data or reinstalling may ask for a new choice. Analytics retention follows the Google Analytics property’s configured retention settings and Google’s service policies; aggregated reports may be retained longer than individual event data. Contact us with questions or requests relating to retained data. Optional exports or linked services, if introduced, will be assessed and disclosed when they change how your information is handled.

Connection security

Android version 3.0.3 requires HTTPS for in-app portal pages and native download requests, disables unencrypted HTTP connections and uses standard server-certificate validation. Invalid certificates are not deliberately bypassed. An external browser or email app handles links and messages opened outside My NUST under its own security settings. Older app versions may have different connection settings; keep the app up to date. These protections do not guarantee the security of a compromised device or a third-party service.

App update reminders

The Android app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available for your installation. A reminder offers “Update now” or “Later”; the same version is not prompted again for at least 24 hours. A manual check is available in Settings. Google Play handles download and installation under its own policies. Update checks do not require analytics consent.

Permissions

Notification permission lets the app display updates. Notifications and the Firebase services supporting them are separate from the optional analytics choice; declining analytics does not disable all app networking or Firebase messaging services. Optional biometric authentication uses the device's authentication system; the app does not receive or store fingerprint or face templates. File handling is used for downloads you request and opening or sharing files. The app does not require broad photo or video library access for its downloads feature.

Feedback

If you send feedback through your email app, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and any name, message or attachments. The in-app feedback form prepares this information in your email app; you decide whether to send it. Opening the email composer is not confirmation that a message was delivered. Do not send portal passwords. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you can contact us to request deletion.

Deleting saved data

In Settings, open “Manage saved data” and select portal connection, GPA planner, absolute planner, app downloads and/or preferences and news cache. Deleting preferences or selecting all categories also disables optional analytics and resets its local app instance identifier. Selecting all resets the listed local app categories. Portal connection deletion removes saved credentials, shared WebView cookies and cached academic summaries. Planner deletion includes imported terms and your edits. Download deletion targets files managed by the app, not your entire public Downloads folder.

Local deletion cannot remove university records, your official portal account, provider logs, previously shared files or copies exported to other locations. Operating-system backups are controlled by your platform. For questions or deletion requests relating to support data, email HexagonePk@gmail.com. The app does not create a separate Hexagone student account.

Website

This landing page has no account form, advertising or analytics script. Firebase Hosting and linked services may process technical request information when you visit. We do not sell portal credentials or academic summaries, or use them for advertising.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold. Contact us with your request; official university data requests must go to the university.
